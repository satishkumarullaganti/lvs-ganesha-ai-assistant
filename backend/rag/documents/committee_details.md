## Committee Responsibilities

### President
Provides overall leadership and coordination for the festival.

### Secretary
Coordinates festival communications, meetings, documentation, and general administration.

### Cultural Coordinator
Coordinates cultural programs, participant registrations, performance details, and event arrangements.

### Volunteer Coordinator
Coordinates volunteers and assigns responsibilities during festival activities.

### Ganesha Bringing Coordinator
Coordinates the arrangements for bringing the Ganesha idol for the festival.

### Mahaprasadam / Annaprasada Coordinator
Coordinates Annaprasada preparation, distribution, volunteers, and related arrangements.

### Decoration Coordinator
Coordinates festival decorations and related arrangements.

### Daily Flowers Coordinator
Coordinates the arrangement of daily flowers for the pooja and decorations.

### Daily Prasad Coordinator
Coordinates the preparation and distribution of daily prasad.

### Daily Rangoli Coordinator
Coordinates the daily rangoli arrangements at the festival venue.

### Games Coordinator
Coordinates games and related activities during the festival.

### Documentary Show Coordinator
Coordinates the documentary screening event.

### Ganesha Fund Collection Coordinator
Coordinates the collection of Ganesha festival donations and funds.

### Food Coupons Coordinator
Coordinates the issuing and management of food coupons for the festival.

### Food Stall Coordinator
Coordinates the food stall arrangements during the festival.

### Bhajan Coordinator
Coordinates the bhajan sessions during the festival.

### Application Support Coordinator
Provides support for the LVS Ganesha AI Assistant application used during the festival.
