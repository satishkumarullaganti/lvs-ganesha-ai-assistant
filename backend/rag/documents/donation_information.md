# LVS Excellency Ganesha Festival - Donation Information

## About Donations

Residents and well-wishers can support the LVS Excellency Ganesha Festival through donations.

Donations help support the planning and execution of festival activities and related arrangements.

Residents can access the Donation feature through the application.

---

# 🪔 Ganesh Idol Information

## Ganesh Idol

The Ganesh Idol is the central focus of the LVS Excellency Ganesha Festival.

The Ganesh Idol will be installed as part of the Ganesh Chaturthi celebrations.

According to the festival schedule:

- **Date:** 14 September 2026
- **Event:** Ganapathi Idol Installation
- **Time:** 08:00 AM
- **Location:** Party Hall

The festival will conclude with Ganesh Visarjan on 19 September 2026.

# Making a Donation

Residents can use the Donation feature in the application to provide their donation details and complete the donation process.

The exact donation fields and payment options are handled by the application.

The AI assistant should provide information about the donation process only when that information is available in the knowledge base.

---

# Donation Process

The general donation flow is:

Resident  
→ Open Donation  
→ Enter Required Details  
→ Select Donation Amount  
→ Complete Payment  
→ Donation Confirmation

The exact steps may depend on the payment method and application implementation.

---

# Donation Amount

Residents may choose the donation amount according to the options provided by the application.

If a resident asks about a minimum or maximum donation amount, the AI assistant should provide the information only if it has been officially specified by the festival committee.

The AI assistant should not invent a minimum or maximum donation amount.

---

# Donation Confirmation

After a successful donation, the application may provide a confirmation or receipt according to the implemented payment process.

Residents should retain their confirmation or receipt for their records.

---

# Donor Information

Information about donors or sponsors should be provided by the AI assistant only when the information has been officially added to the festival knowledge base.

Donations may support festival-related activities and arrangements as determined by the festival committee.

# Privacy and Donor Information

The AI assistant should protect private donor information.

The AI assistant should not provide:

- Personal phone numbers
- Residential addresses
- Bank details
- Payment transaction details
- Private financial information
- Private donation amounts

unless the information has been specifically approved by the festival committee for public display.

Only officially approved donor and sponsor information should be provided to residents.
