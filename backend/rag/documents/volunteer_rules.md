# LVS Excellency Ganesha Festival - Volunteer Rules

## Volunteer Program

Residents who wish to support the LVS Excellency Ganesha Festival can register as volunteers.

Volunteers may be assigned to different festival activities based on the requirements of the event and their availability.

## Volunteer Areas
## Volunteer Area Responsibilities

### Drawing Competition
Assist with participant coordination, seating, materials, and general event support.

### Chess Competition
Assist with participant coordination, seating arrangements, and event organization.

### Carrom Competition
Assist with participant coordination, game-area arrangements, and general event support.

### Tambola Competition
Assist with participant coordination, distribution of materials, and event arrangements.

### Musical Chairs Competition
Assist with participant coordination, arranging the activity area, and general event support.

### Annaprasada Distribution
Assist with food distribution, queue management, coupon verification, cleanliness, and guiding residents.

### Registration Desk
Assist residents with registrations, provide basic information, and coordinate with the appropriate event team.

### Decoration & Setup
Assist with festival decorations, event-area preparation, and setup activities.

### Pooja/Rituals Support
Assist the committee with arrangements and coordination related to pooja and religious activities.

### Cultural Programs Coordination
Assist with participant coordination, stage coordination, performance sequence, and other cultural-program arrangements.

### Donations Desk
Assist with donation-related coordination and guide residents to the appropriate donation process.

### Visarjan Support
Assist with coordination and arrangements related to the Ganesha Visarjan activities.

### Photography/Videography
Assist with photography and videography of festival activities and events.

### Cleanup Crew
Assist with maintaining cleanliness before, during, and after festival activities.

### Other
Residents can specify another area where they would like to volunteer. The requirement will be reviewed and assigned by the volunteer coordinator.

## Volunteer Registration

Residents can register through the LVS AI Assistant.

The volunteer registration may collect:

- Name
- Block
- Flat Number
- Mobile Number
- Preferred Volunteer Area
- Preferred Time Slot
- Available Days

## Volunteer Responsibilities

Volunteers are expected to:

- Report at the assigned location on time.
- Follow instructions from the respective coordinator.
- Support residents and guests politely.
- Maintain discipline and cleanliness.
- Inform the coordinator if they are unable to attend their assigned shift.
- Follow safety instructions during festival activities.
- Cooperate with other volunteers and committee members.

## Annaprasada Volunteers

Volunteers supporting Annaprasada activities may assist with:

- Food distribution
- Queue management
- Coupon verification
- Maintaining cleanliness
- Guiding residents and guests
- Supporting the Annaprasada coordinator

## Cultural Program Volunteers

Volunteers supporting cultural programs may assist with:

- Participant coordination
- Stage coordination
- Managing the performance sequence
- Supporting participants before their performance
- Coordinating music or performance tracks when applicable
- Helping maintain the event schedule

## Crowd Management

Crowd-management volunteers should:

- Guide residents and guests appropriately.
- Keep pathways and designated areas clear.
- Follow instructions from the event coordinator.
- Escalate any safety concerns to the appropriate committee member.

## General Guidelines

Volunteers should always follow the instructions provided by the festival committee and the respective activity coordinator.

The AI assistant should provide volunteer information only from the official volunteer guidelines available in the knowledge base.
