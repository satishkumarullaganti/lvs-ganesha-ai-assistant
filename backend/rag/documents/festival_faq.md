# Cultural Program FAQs

### Are cultural programs a competition?

No.

The cultural programs are festival activities and are **not competitions**.

They are intended to provide residents with an opportunity to participate and showcase their talents.

---

### What types of cultural performances are available?

The cultural program may include:

- Solo Dance
- Group Dance
- Solo Singing
- Group Singing
- Solo Song
- Group Song
- Instrumental Performance
- Skit
- Other Cultural Performance

The final list may be updated by the festival committee.

---

### Do I need to upload my performance track during registration?

No.

The performance track upload is **optional during registration**.

A participant can provide the track later before the event.

---

### Can I upload a track later?

Yes.

Participants who do not have their track ready during registration can provide it later before the event.

The final track should be submitted within any deadline communicated by the festival organizers.

---

### Is a track required for every cultural performance?

No.

Track upload is optional.

Participants who do not require a supporting track do not need to upload one.

---

### Can I upload a track for a dance performance?

Yes.

A supporting performance track can be uploaded for dance performances.

However, the upload is optional during registration.

---

### Can I upload a track for singing?

Yes.

A supporting audio, karaoke, instrumental, or other suitable track can be uploaded if required.

Track upload is optional.

---

### Can a group participate in cultural programs?

Yes.

Group performances such as Group Dance and Group Singing can be registered.

The person registering the performance may be asked to provide the details of the participating group members.

---

### What information is required for cultural registration?

Depending on the performance type, the application may collect:

- Participant Name
- Block
- Flat Number
- Mobile Number
- Performance Type
- Performance Name
- Solo or Group Performance
- Group Member Details
- Approximate Performance Duration
- Supporting Track, if available
- Other performance-related information

The exact fields may depend on the selected performance type.

# Competition FAQs

### What competitions are available?

The current competition and activity categories include:

- Drawing Competition
- Chess Competition
- Carrom Competition
- Tambola Competition
- Musical Chairs Competition

The festival schedule may contain additional competition activities.

---

### What are the basic Chess rules?

Players should follow standard chess rules unless the festival organizers announce modified rules.

Players take turns making moves and should maintain good sportsmanship.

The objective is to checkmate the opponent's king.

The festival committee will confirm the tournament format, age categories, time control, venue, and other specific rules.

---

### What are the basic Carrom rules?

Players should follow standard carrom rules unless modified rules are announced by the organizers.

Players take turns and use the striker to pocket their coins.

Players should not disturb other participants and should maintain good sportsmanship.

The festival committee will confirm the specific match format and scoring system.

---

### What are the basic Tambola rules?

Participants should listen to the numbers called by the organizer and mark the corresponding numbers on their tickets.

If a participant completes an announced winning pattern, they should inform the organizers immediately.

The organizer will verify the claim.

Winning patterns and prize arrangements will be announced by the organizers.

---

### What are the basic Musical Chairs rules?

Participants move around the chairs while music is playing.

When the music stops, participants try to sit on an available chair.

A participant who does not get a chair may be eliminated according to the event format.

Participants should not push, pull, or intentionally hurt another participant.

---

### What are the basic Drawing Competition rules?

Participants should complete their drawing within the time provided by the organizers.

Participants should follow the announced theme and use only permitted materials.

Participants should work independently and should not copy another participant's work.

The final competition details will be provided by the festival committee.

# Annaprasada FAQs

### How does Annaprasada booking work?

The general process is:

Resident Booking
→ QR Coupon Generated
→ Volunteer Scans QR
→ Coupon Validated
→ Coupon Redeemed

---

### Do I get a QR code after booking Annaprasada?

Yes.

A successful Annaprasada booking generates a digital QR coupon.

---

### What should I do with my Annaprasada QR code?

Present the QR code to the volunteer during Annaprasada distribution.

The volunteer will scan the QR code for verification.

---

### Can I use the same Annaprasada QR code twice?

No.

A successfully redeemed coupon cannot be redeemed again.

The system is designed to prevent duplicate redemption.

---

### When is Annaprasada distributed?

Annaprasada Distribution is included in the festival schedule.

Residents should refer to the latest official festival schedule for the date and timing.
