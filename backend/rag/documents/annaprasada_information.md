# LVS Excellency Ganesha Festival - Annaprasada Information

## About Annaprasada

Annaprasada will be provided to residents and eligible participants as part of the LVS Excellency Ganesha Festival activities.

Residents can use the Annaprasada feature in the application to make a booking and receive a digital coupon.

---

## Annaprasada Booking

Residents can access Annaprasada through the Annaprasada feature card in the application.

The booking process may require resident information as defined by the application.

After a successful booking, the system generates a digital QR coupon.

The QR code is used for verification during Annaprasada distribution.

---

## QR Coupon

A successful Annaprasada booking generates a unique QR code.

The resident should present the QR code when collecting Annaprasada.

The volunteer can scan the QR code using the volunteer scanning functionality.

The system verifies the coupon before allowing redemption.

---

## Coupon Redemption

During Annaprasada distribution:

1. The resident presents the QR coupon.
2. A volunteer scans the QR code.
3. The system verifies the coupon.
4. If the coupon is valid and unused, it can be redeemed.
5. The system records the redemption.

---

## Duplicate Redemption

A redeemed Annaprasada coupon cannot be redeemed again.

If a resident attempts to use the same QR coupon a second time, the system should identify that the coupon has already been redeemed and prevent duplicate redemption.

The volunteer should not manually override the duplicate-redemption protection unless an authorized festival administrator provides a separate process.

---

## Annaprasada Distribution

Annaprasada distribution will take place according to the festival schedule.

The Schedule feature and official festival schedule should be used to determine the latest published distribution timings.

The AI assistant should not invent Annaprasada timings.

---

## Annaprasada Volunteers

Volunteers may assist with:

- QR coupon scanning
- Coupon verification
- Queue management
- Annaprasada distribution
- Guiding residents
- Maintaining cleanliness
- Supporting the Annaprasada coordinator

Volunteers should follow the instructions provided by the Annaprasada coordinator.

---

## Resident Guidelines

Residents should:

- Complete their booking before attempting to collect Annaprasada.
- Keep their QR coupon available when approaching the distribution area.
- Present the QR code to the volunteer for scanning.
- Follow the queue and instructions provided by volunteers.
- Avoid attempting to use a QR coupon that has already been redeemed.
- Contact the appropriate festival coordinator if there is a problem with their booking or coupon.

---

## Important Information

The exact Annaprasada distribution timings, menu, eligibility, booking limits, and other arrangements will be based on the information officially provided by the festival committee.

If specific information has not been published or added to the knowledge base, the AI assistant should clearly state that the information is not currently available.

The AI assistant should not invent:

- Annaprasada timings
- Menu items
- Coupon limits
- Eligibility rules
- Booking limits
- Distribution locations
- Other festival-specific Annaprasada rules

Any future updates from the festival committee should be reflected in this document.

---

## Technical Transaction Flow

The Annaprasada application flow is:

Resident Booking
→ QR Coupon Generated
→ Volunteer Scans QR
→ Coupon Validated
→ Coupon Redeemed

A successfully redeemed coupon cannot be redeemed again.

The AI assistant should provide general information about this process, while the actual booking, QR generation, scanning, validation, and redemption are handled by the application.
