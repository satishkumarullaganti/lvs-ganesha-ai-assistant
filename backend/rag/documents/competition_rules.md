# LVS Excellency Ganesha Festival - Competition Information and Rules

## Competitions

The LVS Excellency Ganesha Festival may include various competitions and fun activities for residents and participants.

The competitions currently available through the festival application include:

- Drawing Competition
- Chess Competition
- Carrom Competition
- Tambola Competition
- Musical Chairs Competition

The generic rules below are provided as general guidance. The festival committee may modify these rules based on the actual event arrangements.

---

# Drawing Competition

## General Rules

- Participants should complete their drawing within the time provided by the organizers.
- The drawing should be based on the theme announced by the organizers, if a theme is specified.
- Participants should bring any required drawing materials unless the organizers provide them.
- Participants should use only the materials permitted by the organizers.
- Participants should work independently.
- Participants should not copy another participant's work.
- Completed drawings should be submitted to the organizers before the specified deadline.
- Participants should follow the instructions given by the event coordinator.
- The decision of the organizers/judges regarding the competition result will be final, subject to the festival committee's rules.

## Final Details

The festival committee will confirm:

- Age categories
- Drawing theme
- Time limit
- Materials allowed
- Venue
- Reporting time
- Judging criteria
- Prizes

# Chess Competition

## General Rules

- Each player should follow the standard rules of chess unless the organizers announce modified rules.
- Players should take turns making one move at a time.
- A player should not touch or move pieces unnecessarily.
- Players should not disturb or distract other participants.
- Players should follow the time control, if a chess clock is used.
- A player should inform the organizer if there is a dispute or problem during the game.
- Players should maintain good sportsmanship.
- The result of the game should be reported to the event coordinator when required.
- Participants should follow the tournament format announced by the organizers.

## Basic Chess Objective

The objective is to checkmate the opponent's king.

A checkmate occurs when the king is under attack and there is no legal move available to remove the threat.

# Carrom Competition

## General Rules

- Players should follow the standard rules of carrom unless the organizers announce modified rules.
- Players should take turns according to the format announced by the organizers.
- The striker should be placed within the permitted area before making a shot.
- Players should use the striker to pocket their coins.
- Players should not move or touch the carrom board unnecessarily.
- Players should not disturb other players during their turn.
- Fouls should be handled according to the rules announced by the organizers.
- Players should maintain good sportsmanship.
- Any dispute should be referred to the event coordinator.

## Basic Objective

The objective is to pocket the required carrom coins according to the game format.

The winner will be determined according to the scoring and match format announced by the organizers.

The festival committee will confirm:

- Singles or doubles format
- Age categories
- Number of players
- Match format
- Scoring system
- Reporting time

# Tambola Competition

## General Rules

- Each participant should have a valid Tambola ticket/card as provided by the organizers.
- Participants should listen carefully to the numbers announced by the caller.
- Participants should mark called numbers correctly on their ticket.
- Participants should not mark numbers that have not been called.
- A participant who believes they have completed a winning pattern should immediately inform the organizers.
- The organizer should verify the ticket before declaring the claim valid.
- A claim made after the relevant number or next number has been called may be handled according to the event rules.
- Participants should not interfere with another participant's ticket.
- Participants should maintain fair play and good sportsmanship.

## Common Winning Patterns

Depending on the format, the organizers may announce prizes for patterns such as:

- Early Five
- Top Line
- Middle Line
- Bottom Line
- Full House

The actual winning patterns and prizes will be announced by the organizers before the game.

## Important

The festival committee will decide the official winning patterns, prize structure, claim rules, and number-calling procedure for the event.

# Musical Chairs Competition

## General Rules

- Chairs are arranged in a circle or another arrangement decided by the organizers.
- Participants move around the chairs while music is playing.
- When the music stops, participants should try to sit on an available chair.
- A participant who does not get a chair may be eliminated from that round.
- The number of chairs may be reduced as participants are eliminated.
- The game continues until the final participant remains.
- Participants should not push, pull, or intentionally hurt another participant.
- Participants should follow the instructions of the event coordinator.
- Participants should maintain good sportsmanship.

## Safety

- Participants should avoid aggressive running or pushing.
- The playing area should be kept clear.
- Children should participate only in the appropriate age category if age categories are used.
- The event coordinator may stop or modify the activity if there is a safety concern.
