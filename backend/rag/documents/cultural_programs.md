# LVS Excellency Ganesha Festival - Cultural Programs

## About Cultural Programs

The LVS Excellency Ganesha Festival includes cultural programs that provide residents with an opportunity to participate and showcase their talents.

Cultural programs may include music, dance, instrumental performances, skits, and other suitable performances.

Cultural programs are festival activities and are not treated as competitions.

---

# Cultural Performance Types

Participants may register for one of the available performance categories.

The available categories may include:

- Solo Dance
- Group Dance
- Solo Singing
- Group Singing
- Solo Song
- Group Song
- Instrumental Performance
- Skit
- Other Cultural Performance

The final list of available performance categories may be updated by the festival committee.

---

# Solo Dance

A participant can register individually for a dance performance.

Participants should provide the required performance details during registration.

A performance track may be uploaded if required.

Track upload is optional during registration and can also be provided later before the event.

---

# Group Dance

A group can register for a dance performance.

The participant registering the performance should provide the required group and performance details.

The names of group members may be collected as part of the registration process.

A performance track may be uploaded if required.

Track upload is optional during registration and can be provided later before the event.

---

# Solo Singing

A participant can register individually for a singing performance.

The participant may provide the song or performance details during registration.

A karaoke track, instrumental track, or other supporting audio may be uploaded if required.

Track upload is optional.

Participants may provide the track later before the event.

---

# Other Cultural Performance

Participants may register for another suitable cultural performance that is not included in the listed categories.

The participant should provide details of the proposed performance.

The festival committee may review the proposed performance for suitability and event planning.

A supporting audio track may be uploaded if required.

Track upload is optional and can be provided later before the event.

---

# Performance Track Upload

Participants may upload a supporting performance track for their cultural program.

The track upload is optional during registration.

Participants do not need to have the track ready at the time of registration.

A participant can provide the performance track later before the scheduled event.

The upload option may be used for different types of cultural performances, including:

- Singing
- Dance
- Group Dance
- Group Singing
- Instrumental Performance
- Skit
- Other performances where supporting audio is required

The uploaded file should be a suitable audio file supported by the festival application.

The festival organizers may specify the accepted file formats and maximum file size.

Participants should ensure that the uploaded track is the correct version that will be used for their performance.

If a participant does not require a supporting track, they do not need to upload one.

---

# Cultural Program Registration

Residents can register for cultural programs through the LVS AI Assistant or the Cultural Programs registration section of the application.

The registration process may collect information such as:

- Participant Name
- Block
- Flat Number
- Mobile Number
- Performance Type
- Performance Name
- Solo or Group Performance
- Group Member Details, where applicable
- Approximate Performance Duration
- Supporting Track, if available
- Other performance-related information required by the organizers

The exact fields shown in the application may depend on the selected performance type.

---

# Group Performance

For group performances, the person registering the performance may be asked to provide the details of the participating members.

The group should coordinate internally and ensure that the information provided during registration is accurate.

The festival organizers may contact the registered participant for additional information if required.

---

# Performance Track Submission Before the Event

Participants who do not have their performance track ready during registration may provide it later.

The festival application may provide a separate option for uploading or submitting the track before the event.

Participants should submit their final track within the deadline communicated by the festival organizers.

If a track submission deadline is announced, participants should follow that deadline.

---

# Cultural Program Guidelines

Participants are expected to:

- Register with accurate information.
- Provide the correct performance details.
- Follow the schedule communicated by the festival organizers.
- Report to the designated location at the announced time.
- Follow instructions from the cultural program coordinator.
- Cooperate with volunteers and event coordinators.
- Maintain appropriate behavior during the event.
- Respect other participants and performers.
- Ensure that their performance is suitable for the festival environment.
- Submit supporting audio or other required materials within the announced deadline.

---

# Performance Duration

The festival committee may specify a maximum duration for individual performances.

Participants should follow the duration communicated by the cultural program coordinator.

If a specific performance duration has not yet been announced, the AI assistant should not invent one.

---

# Cultural Program Schedule

Cultural performances will be arranged according to the festival schedule prepared by the organizers.

The performance order and reporting time may be communicated to participants before the event.

Participants should check the latest festival schedule and instructions from the cultural program coordinator.

---

# Important Information

Cultural programs are festival activities and are not competitions.

There is no competition judging process associated with cultural program registration unless the festival committee announces a separate activity in the future.

The cultural program registration process is intended to help the organizers plan performances, participants, stage arrangements, and event schedules.

---

# Final Rules and Updates

The festival committee may update:

- Performance categories
- Registration requirements
- Performance duration
- Track submission deadlines
- Venue
- Reporting time
- Performance sequence
- Technical requirements
- Other event arrangements

The latest information officially communicated by the festival committee should be treated as the final information.

The AI assistant should not invent cultural program rules, timings, deadlines, or requirements.

If specific information is not available in the knowledge base, the AI assistant should clearly state that the information has not yet been confirmed or published by the festival committee.
